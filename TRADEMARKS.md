## English

### The short version

The **source code** is free: GPL-3.0. You may use, study, change, build and redistribute it under that license. The **name "HermoPDF" and the logo** are different: they are our marks, and they are **not** licensed under the GPL (the GPL does not grant rights to use names and marks; see GPL-3.0, section 7(e)). If you distribute a modified build, give it **your own name and logo**.

Status: "HermoPDF" is a working name. It is an unregistered mark claimed by the project's author (NorthLightPixels / Fediac). Registration may be sought later. Clearance is not complete, so the name may change; if it does, this policy applies to the new name.

### You can, without asking

- Say you use or work with HermoPDF, link to the project, and write reviews, tutorials or comparisons using the name truthfully.
- Use phrases like "compatible with HermoPDF" or "a plugin for HermoPDF" when it is accurate, and make clear you are not the project.
- Redistribute the **official, unmodified** installers exactly as released, with their SHA-256 values, and point to the official site for updates.
- Build from the source for your own use and describe it as "built from the HermoPDF source".
- Use screenshots of the official application in articles and videos.

### You need our written permission to

- Distribute a **modified** build under the name "HermoPDF", or with our logo, icons or look-alikes.
- Use a name, logo, domain, store listing or social-media handle that includes "HermoPDF" or is confusingly similar (for example "HermoPDF Pro", "HermoPDF Official", "Hermo PDF Free Download").
- Suggest that your product, site or service is official, endorsed by or affiliated with us.
- Sell licenses, keys or support under our name. Only the project's official channels sell official Pro licenses.

### Forks and modified builds

You are free to fork. Before you publish a fork:

1. Pick **a different name** and a different logo and icon. Do not use "HermoPDF" in a way that suggests it is us.
2. Remove our logos and icons (logo and icon files are **excluded from the GPL grant**).
3. State clearly that your project is a fork of HermoPDF and link to the original. "Based on HermoPDF" is fine.
4. Keep the GPL-3.0 license, copyright notices and the source offer.
5. Unlocking Pro features: the Pro code is open source, so you may change or remove the license check in your own fork. Your build must not claim to be an official license, must not use our license-signing keys, and must not present itself as the official build.

Unmodified redistribution of the official installer is allowed, as above.

### Using the logo and name correctly

- Use the name as "HermoPDF" (one word; capital U, P, D and F), not as a verb or plural.
- Do not alter, animate or recolor the logo, and give it clear space.
- Do not use the logo in a way that implies we sponsor or endorse you.

### Reporting misuse

If you see someone using the name or logo in a misleading way, tell us at `northlightstudios25@gmail.com`. We prefer a friendly request first.
