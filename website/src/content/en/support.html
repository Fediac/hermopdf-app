How to write a good bug report

  1. Version.The version number in the app's About window (e.g. {{cfg:release.version}}) and your Windows version (10 or 11).
  2. What did you do?Step by step: "I opened the PDF, clicked Compress, typed 1…"
  3. What did you expect, and what happened?Copy any error message exactly or attach a screenshot (with personal details covered).
  4. Does it happen every time?Only with one file, or with every file?
