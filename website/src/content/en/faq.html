General

Is HermoPDF really free? What's the catch?

Yes, for everyday PDF work: editing, compressing to a target size, merging, splitting, signing and basic conversions are free, with no watermark, ads, daily limit, trial or account. An optional one-time Lifetime Pro adds OCR, batch processing and saved workflows and funds development.

Which operating systems does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). There is no macOS, Linux or mobile version.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The app only goes online if you turn on the optional update check. If you turn on the Claude connection, Claude Desktop uses its own connection; see Claude connection.

What language is the app in?

The interface is in Turkish. OCR (a Pro feature) recognises Turkish and English text.

Privacy and security

Are my documents uploaded to a server?

No, everything happens on your computer. The one exception is the Claude connection, if you turn it on: what Claude reads goes to Anthropic. Details

Does the app collect usage data (telemetry)?

No. The update check is opt-in; if you enable it, the app downloads a small signed file from our site at most once a day. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address and the app version. Privacy policy

Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

The app is published without a code-signing certificate; it is not a virus warning. Verify the SHA-256 checksum, then click "More info" → "Run anyway". Step-by-step instructions

Is redacted text really removed?

Yes. The page becomes a high-resolution (200 DPI) image and the text in the marked area is dropped from the file. How it works

Pro and licensing

What does Lifetime Pro add? Is it a subscription?

Pro adds OCR (text recognition), batch processing and saved workflows. "Lifetime" means one payment, no subscription, no auto-renewal and no time limit: you keep using the Pro features for life.

Who takes the payment?

Freemius, as Merchant of Record, takes the payment. We never see your card details.

There is a payment on my statement I don't recognise. What is it?

Your HermoPDF payment is taken by Freemius, so your statement may show "FREEMIUS* HERMOPDF" or "FREEMIUS SOFTWARE" instead of our name. Check the receipt e-mail from Freemius; if it matches, that is your purchase. If you still don't recognise it, write to us and we will help (see the refund policy).

I got a second key and an account from Freemius. Which key do I use?

Freemius may create its own license key and an account (with a temporary password) for you. There are two different keys: the Freemius key, which starts with sk_, is for Freemius's own use, such as recovering your purchase. The key that opens the app is the HermoPDF license from our e-mail. Use that one in the app.

My license e-mail didn't arrive.

It is sent once, right after purchase, and can land in your Spam / Junk folder; please check there first. If it is not there, use the Lost my license page to have it sent again.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. A full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. Refund policy

I lost my license key. What now?

Enter the e-mail you used at checkout on the Lost my license page; your license is sent to that address again.

Does the license make the app go online?

Activation works offline; the key is verified by its signature. If you enabled the update check, the app downloads a signed list of refunded licenses at most once a week. Your license key and e-mail are not sent; only your IP address and the app version are visible.

Open source

Why does an open-source program have a paid version?

GPL-3.0 allows paid distribution. Pro is how a single developer keeps the program ad-free and free to use. The code is open to everyone; the "HermoPDF" name and logo are reserved trademarks. Details
