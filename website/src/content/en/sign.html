Three ways to create your signature

Draw

With a mouse, touchscreen or pen.

Type

Type your name; it becomes a signature in the font you choose.

From an image

Pick a photo of your signature on paper; the background is cleaned up.

  • Save your signature and initials; they stay on this computer only. Dates and stamps can be added too.

Important: this is a visual signature, not a qualified e-signature

The signature is an image placed on the page, not a qualified electronic signature or a certificate-based digital signature. If you need one, use your e-signature provider's tool.
