How to do it

  1. Open the scanned PDF.If there is no selectable text, the app offers OCR.
  2. Tools → OCR.Choose the language: Turkish + English (default), Turkish only or English only.
  3. Wait, then use it.The text is added as an invisible layer. Search and copy it, or turn it into editable text with PDF → Word.

Language data is included: the OCR language files ship with the app; nothing is downloaded.

OCR is a Pro feature. The free version opens scanned PDFs and tells you when a page needs OCR; running OCR (whole document, chosen pages or an area) needs Lifetime Pro.

Honest note: OCR is only as good as the scan. Skewed, blurry or handwritten pages may contain errors; check important documents by eye.
