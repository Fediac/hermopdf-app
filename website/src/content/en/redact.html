True redaction

A black box is not enough.

A black rectangle over text does not hide it; the text underneath can often still be selected and searched. HermoPDF actually removes the content you mark.

How it works

  1. Mark the areas to hide.ID numbers, IBANs, addresses, signatures… Marks are only marks at first; you can undo them.
  2. Click "Apply redaction".Once you confirm, the pages become high-resolution (200 DPI) images, the marked areas are painted black and the text there is dropped from the file. Other text stays selectable.
  3. Share the copy.Save the redacted document under a new name; keep the original.

Try it yourself: after saving, try to select or search the redacted text. You will not find it, because it is no longer there.

Honest note: once saved, redaction cannot be reversed in that file; check the result before sharing. A redacted page becomes an image, so its links may be lost and the file may grow a little. Draw the box so it fully covers the text.
