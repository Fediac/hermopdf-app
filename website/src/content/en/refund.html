14 days, unconditional, full refund

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of buying Lifetime Pro, without giving any reason. No questions, no deductions.

How do I ask for a refund?

Either way works:

  • Write to {{cfg:contactEmail}} from the e-mail address you used at checkout. Adding the transaction number (on your Freemius receipt) speeds things up.
  • Or use the contact details in the receipt e-mail you received for the purchase.

When do I get my money back?

Refunds are made through Freemius, our Merchant of Record, to the payment method you used. Card refunds usually take a few business days to show up, depending on your bank.

What happens to the license?

The license key from a refunded purchase becomes invalid and is added to the revocation list; apps with the update check enabled switch off the Pro features unlocked by that key once they download the list. Everything in Free keeps working and none of your documents are affected.

After 14 days

Write to us anyway. If something went wrong, we will do our best to fix it. Your statutory consumer rights always apply.
