Claude connection

If you use Claude Desktop, you can let Claude see your open document: "Where is the payment date?", "Highlight the total on page 3", "Shrink this under 2 MB". It is completely off unless you turn it on.

If you turn it on, what data goes where?

Whatever Claude reads through HermoPDF (page text, page images, search results, document names) goes to Anthropic inside your own Claude conversation and is subject to your Claude account terms. It does not come to us; we cannot see it.

For confidential documents, keep the connection off.

You stay in control

  • It cannot save on its own. The app asks you first.
  • It cannot apply redactions. It can only mark areas; applying needs your confirmation.
  • Edits can be undone. Each edit shows up as a normal step; Undo reverts it.
  • Nothing goes to our servers. The connection runs through a small local server (MCP) on your computer.

How to turn it on (the connection is free)

  1. Install Claude Desktop and sign in.Claude usage counts against your own Claude plan.
  2. In the Araçlar (Tools) tab, click "Claude'a bağla" (Connect to Claude).Accept the window explaining what will be shared and the connection is set up.
  3. Turn it off any time.Click the Claude indicator in the app.
