Questions about pricing

Why does a free program have a Pro version?

HermoPDF is open source and made by a single developer. We do not want ads and we will not sell your data; Pro is the honest way to keep the program going. Without Pro, everyday PDF work (editing, compressing, merging, splitting, signing, basic conversions) stays free; Pro adds OCR, batch processing and saved workflows.

Can I get an invoice? How is VAT handled?

Freemius sells the license as Merchant of Record and handles the payment. You see the total at checkout before you pay; for tax and invoice details, see the receipt you get from Freemius.

What if I lose my license?

Enter the e-mail address you used at checkout on the Lost my license page and your license is sent to that address again.
