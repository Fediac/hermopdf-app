Word → PDF

Your Word document as a PDF, intact

Two engines keep images in place and fonts unchanged.

{{t:ctaDownload}}
{{> ico-word}}

1. If Microsoft Word is installed

Word's own PDF engine is used; the result matches "Save as PDF" in Word. .doc, .docx and other formats Word can open are supported.

Your open Word windows are left alone.

{{> ico-pages}}

2. If Word is not installed

The built-in engine converts .docx documents to PDF directly. No Office needed.

It works with the .docx family (.docx, .docm, .dotx). Complex layouts may not be as exact as with Word.

The other direction, too

  • PDF → Word (.docx): make the text editable. For scans, run OCR first.
  • PDF → images: save pages as PNG or JPEG.
  • Images → PDF: collect photos and scans into one PDF.
  • Text → PDF: turn a plain text file into a PDF.
